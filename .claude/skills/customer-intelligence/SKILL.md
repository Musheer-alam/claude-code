# Skill: Customer Intelligence

## Purpose
Build customer understanding and a citable language bank. (P2, P4 — TLPC VERIFIED: n≥5 interviews, "bloated after lunch" > "gut health optimisation". SYS improvement: add breadth sources TLPC never named.)

## Inputs
Discovery (product, audience hypothesis, competitors); supplied interviews/transcripts/surveys/support exports/review exports.

## Process
1. **Inventory sources** and record status: INTERVIEW DATA AVAILABLE (n) / NOT AVAILABLE. Never simulate an interview.
2. **Collect** (priority order): supplied interviews → supplied surveys/tickets → on-site reviews → marketplace/review-platform reviews (prefer 3★ and 1–2★ for objections, 4–5★ for outcomes) → competitor reviews (same category) → Reddit/forums (search the *problem*, not the brand) → YouTube/social comments. Target ≥30 verbatims when public data allows; at least 5 distinct sources if possible.
3. **Bank** each quote exactly in `verbatim-bank.md` (V-nnn) with source, type, context, theme, confidence, public-usability. Private interview lines are `Usable in public copy: NO` unless consent is recorded.
4. **Code themes**: problem/symptom, desired outcome, trigger, fear, objection, alternative, decision criterion, delight. Count frequencies.
5. **Jobs-to-be-done**: "When <situation>, I want to <motivation>, so I can <outcome>" — only from evidence.
6. **Awareness** (unaware → most aware) and **sophistication** (1 new claim … 5 jaded) with cited signals (e.g. reviews comparing 4 brands ⇒ high sophistication).
7. **Segments**: note groups that sound higher-quality (long-term use, repeat, referrals) vs wrong-fit (returns, "not for my X"). This feeds positioning and the wrong-fit filter.
8. **Language highlights**: 10–20 phrases that are first-person, situation-specific, time/place anchored; contrast with category jargon.

## Decision rules
- A verbatim without a citable source is discarded.
- Frequency beats vividness for themes; vividness is fine for hero candidates if the theme is frequent.
- If n≥5 interviews are not met: confidence ≤ MEDIUM for motivations/JTBD; say so.
- Competitor reviews describe the category's pains (useful) but not this brand's performance.

## Outputs
`02-customer/customer-intelligence.md`, `02-customer/verbatim-bank.md`, evidence E-100–E-199.

## Failure conditions
No VOC accessible → `PUBLIC VERBATIMS: NONE FOUND` + sources attempted; recommend review export or 5 customer calls (NICE-TO-HAVE unless strategy can't proceed).

## Quality checks
- [ ] Exact quotes, cited, no composites
- [ ] Theme counts present
- [ ] Interview status explicit; n≥5 met/not met
- [ ] Objections with frequency
- [ ] Awareness & sophistication evidenced
