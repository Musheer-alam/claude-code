# Skill: Competitive Intelligence

## Purpose
Know what the category already claims so the page does not repeat exhausted promises, and find differentiation the business can credibly own. (TLPC VERIFIED: competitive teardown as a Tier-01 inclusion; method not published → SYS method below.)

## Inputs
Discovery facts (category, price band, competitors spotted); optionally customer research (if already written) for white-space cross-checks.

## Process
1. **Set**: 3–6 direct competitors (same job, same price band), 1–3 indirect (different product, same job), the category leader, and the alternative of doing nothing/DIY. Find them via the customer's problem phrasing in search, marketplace "related", ad libraries, and review mentions.
2. **Teardown** each landing page/PDP on mobile: hero headline (verbatim), subheadline, offer & price, core promise, proof types and numbers shown, mechanism story, objections handled (FAQ topics), risk reversal terms, CTA text and structure, discount/urgency devices. Screenshot the first viewport. Cite URL + access date.
3. **Exhaustion map**: which promises/proof/language appear on ≥50% of pages → generic, invisible. List overused phrases verbatim.
4. **White space**: customer pains/outcomes (from VOC) that no competitor addresses; proof types nobody shows; mechanisms nobody explains; audiences nobody names.
5. **Credibility filter**: keep only angles with evidence they are true for this business (spec, certification, data, policy) — otherwise mark "hypothesis — needs evidence".
6. **Sophistication** (1–5): 1 = first claim works; 3 = need a mechanism; 5 = buyers jaded, need identification/experience.

## Decision rules
- Competitor claims are OBSERVED claims; truth is UNKNOWN.
- Substitution test: if a candidate hero would read as true on a competitor page, it is not differentiation.
- Price comparisons only with dated, cited prices.

## Outputs
`04-market/competitive-intelligence.md`; evidence E-300–E-399; `04-market/snapshots/`.

## Failure conditions
Too few identifiable competitors → widen to indirect/alternatives and say so.

## Quality checks
- [ ] Matrix complete for each competitor, cited
- [ ] Exhaustion + overused phrases listed
- [ ] Differentiation angles tagged credible (with evidence) vs hypothesis
- [ ] Sophistication level with reason
