# Skill: Page Archetype

## Purpose
Choose the page type from evidence, never fashion.

## Inputs
Strategy (job, message, objections), positioning, audit (top leaks), customer awareness/sophistication, traffic temperature and device split.

## Process
Score each archetype 0–3 against the inputs; write For/Against with evidence; choose; justify vs runner-up; list risks.

## Decision rules (heuristics — each must be confirmed by project evidence)
| Signal | Leans toward |
|---|---|
| Hot traffic (brand search, retargeting, email), high awareness, simple product | Offer-led / e-commerce PDP |
| Cold paid social, problem-aware, symptom language strong | Problem-solution (or advertorial pre-sell if very cold and the category is sceptical) |
| High scepticism / regulated / "does it work?" top objection | Proof-led or problem-solution with early substantiation |
| Buyers comparing named alternatives (high sophistication) | Comparison |
| Fit uncertainty (sizing, suitability, "is it for me?") drives returns | Quiz/diagnostic or PDP with strong self-qualification |
| High price / complex purchase / B2B / service | Lead-generation or application; long-form if many objections must be cleared before the ask |
| Selective offer, capacity-constrained, quality of lead matters more than volume | Application |
| Brand trust rests on a person, story-driven category | Founder-led |
| Mixed signals | Hybrid — name the primary and the borrowed element and why |

- Do not pick an archetype that contradicts the primary conversion (e.g. advertorial when the job is direct checkout for hot traffic).
- Do not choose "long-form" just because objections exist; choose it when objections must be cleared *before* the CTA and the audience will read (evidence: scroll depth, time on page, category).

## Outputs
`08-architecture/page-archetype.md` + DECISION entry (provenance PROJECT-SPECIFIC DECISION).

## Failure conditions
Inputs insufficient to separate two archetypes → choose the lower-risk one, record the other as a test hypothesis.
