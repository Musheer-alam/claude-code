# Conflict Register

> When two agents (or two sources) disagree, the orchestrator records it here instead of silently choosing. `transition` out of STRATEGY is refused while any conflict is OPEN.
> Resolution order: 1 direct behavioural data · 2 direct customer interviews · 3 first-party analytics · 4 primary-source evidence · 5 repeated observed patterns · 6 inference · 7 generic marketing assumptions.
> If evidence is evenly matched and the choice changes strategy materially, ask the user.
