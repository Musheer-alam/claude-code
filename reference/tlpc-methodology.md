# TLPC Methodology — evidence-graded digest

Source: the user's research dossier *"Reverse-Engineering The Landing Page Company"* (claude.ai artifact c9a33f73-41d5-4d87-896d-cf534cb90315, compiled 12 Sep 2026). This digest keeps the dossier's grades. **Use as a methodological reference only.** Nothing here is a fact about any client project, and no TLPC performance figure is evidence for anything.

Grades: **VERIFIED** (directly stated/observed in primary sources) · **OBSERVED** (repeatedly visible) · **INFERRED** (reasoned, not proven) · **UNKNOWN**.

## What is VERIFIED (as stated by TLPC — not as proven practice)
- Offer: "+20% better than what's running today — or we keep working" (labour guarantee, not refund).
- Qualification: 15-field, 5-step application (revenue, ad spend, platform, orders, AOV, CVR, growth blocker, trigger, decision maker, **"top 3 pages that drive most of your revenue"**); a published "who we don't work with" list (incl. no customer conversation in 90 days; briefs with "47 things the page must say").
- Research stack named: n≥5 customer interviews; Meta, Shopify, GA, Microsoft Clarity (heatmaps, funnels, drop-offs, recordings); competitive teardown.
- Three CRO factors: (1) Audience — "the page chooses your customer", discount-led vs brand-led LTV; (2) Language — customer's own words ("bloated after lunch" > "gut health optimisation"); (3) Trust — design credibility as a conversion variable ("will it still exist in 2027?").
- "Wrong win" kill rule: a discount-led variant with higher CVR killed at week 2 on return rate and LTV. (Whether a real engagement: UNKNOWN; figures reused, possibly illustrative.)
- "No testimonials until there's a number next to them."
- Mobile-first; "real copy, real assets" before design sign-off; hand-coded; manual real-device QA by a dedicated QA role as a blocking launch gate.

## What is OBSERVED (across their own site and the client page most plausibly theirs)
- Proof/credentials within the first two screens (4/4 pages).
- Numeric density in claims; explicit objection inventories (FAQ ×11 on The Good Bug PDP); secondary soft conversions (consult, quiz, audit); AOV/pack ladders (4/4).
- **Self-disqualification** of wrong-fit buyers ("not for you if…", routing to another product) on the two highest-attribution pages.
- Expectation-setting to suppress returns ("takes time", trial periods, time-framed testimonials).
- Observed PDP sequence: symptom recognition → commercial choice → legitimacy → self-qualification → mechanism → substantiation → social proof → category differentiation → objection clearance → compliance.
- Absence of any experimentation apparatus (0/4 pages, no tool, no significance language).
- Contradiction: 3 of 4 client pages are discount-led despite the anti-discount philosophy.

## What is INFERRED
- The 3-step public process is a sales artefact; the real workflow is ~13 phases.
- +20% is most likely established by before/after period comparison (confounded), not controlled tests.
- Depth-over-breadth qualitative research (five interviews, no review mining).
- Method tuned for high-scepticism, mid-AOV, repeat-purchase categories (11/16 named clients are ingestibles/personal care).
- Where The Good Bug's lift (if real) came from: objection clearance and expectation-setting more than layout.

## UNKNOWN / weaknesses this system must NOT inherit
- No documented experimentation system, significance thresholds, sample-size method, test duration, or platform.
- No independently verified performance results; portfolio unlinkable; several internal contradictions (tenure, retention, page/client counts).
- Whether n≥5 interviews actually happen per engagement.
- The 47-check checklist contents (the dossier's 25-criteria scorecard is a reconstruction, not TLPC's).
- Copy QA gaps on their own site (typos) — QA must cover copy, not only functionality.

## How agents should use this
- Borrow mechanisms, not claims. Tag any borrowed practice in DECISION entries with its provenance.
- Never cite TLPC numbers (+34%, +27%, ₹1.8k/₹5.4k, +50% …) as expectations for a client page.
- The observed sequence is one hypothesis among many; the architect must justify every section order from project evidence.
